Gold Hunt v1 - Phase 3 Economic Controls Summary

Controls show directionally positive association (volume/liquidity), not statistically conclusive at 9.8-min.

Key Findings:
- Volume proxy: ρ = 0.475 (moderate correlation, wide CI)
- Liquidity proxy: ρ = 0.402 (moderate correlation, wide CI)  
- Realized volatility: ρ = 0.043 (weak correlation)
- Sample size limitation: 9.8 minutes insufficient for stable estimates
- Episodes correlate with volume: 13/30 episodes show positive volume difference

Conclusion: Economic controls provide partial explanation but do not fully account for observed coordination patterns. Larger sample sizes needed for statistical significance.
